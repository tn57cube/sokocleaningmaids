Our Certificates

At Soko Cleaning Maids Ltd, we take training and compliance seriously — especially when it comes to specialist cleaning work. Every member of our team is trained and certified before they're sent out on a job, so you can trust that the work is done safely, correctly, and to a professional standard.


Our COSHH (Control of Substances Hazardous to Health) Awareness Training ensures our staff understand how to store, handle, and use cleaning chemicals safely — protecting both your property and our team from any risk of harm.


For biohazard and trauma cleaning, our team holds C1IC compliance certification, the UK standard for safe handling of bodily fluids, infectious waste, and hazardous contamination. This means we can be trusted with sensitive, high-risk jobs that require discretion, care, and strict adherence to health and safety regulations.


We also train our staff in the safe handling and disposal of needles and sharps, which is essential for jobs in public spaces, after-event cleanups, and any environment where hazardous waste may be present. Ongoing training is part of how we maintain our high standards — if you have any questions about our certifications or want to see documentation before booking a job, just get in touch.
